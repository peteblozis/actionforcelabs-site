📚 Prompt Lessons

A short, hands-on series for learning how to talk to AI clearly. Eight lessons. Each builds on the last. Work through them in order or jump to whichever you need.

← Tester home

How to use this series

Each lesson is short — about 5 minutes to read, 10–15 to practice. Pause and try the examples in your own AI tool. Send feedback to Pete via the tester feedback form. The lessons are revised based on what testers find confusing, so your input directly shapes the final product.
